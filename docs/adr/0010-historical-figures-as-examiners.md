# 0010 — Historical computing pioneers may appear as Examiners; living or recently deceased people may not

**Status:** accepted · 2026-10-14 · complements [ADR-0007](0007-parody-antagonist.md)

## Context
Floor 1's bosses are Ada Lovelace (died 1852) and Charles Babbage (died 1871). ADR-0007 keeps real living people (e.g. tech CEOs) out of the game. Historical figures are a different case, but "historical" needs a line. Several US states protect a person's name and likeness after death; California's right lasts 70 years, and estates do enforce it.

## Decision
- A real person may appear **by name and likeness** only if they died **70 or more years ago**.
- Anyone more recent (e.g. Grace Hopper, died 1992; Edsger Dijkstra, died 2002) appears only as an **original homage character** with a different name and design, unless the estate has cleared it.
- Historical figures are **Examiners**: demanding, proud and fair. They are never mocked or made villains, and they acknowledge the Climber on defeat. Their Tasks are about their real contributions.
- Likenesses are drawn from public-domain portraits; no modern photographs.
- Living people stay excluded (ADR-0007).

## Consequences
- Floor 1 (Lovelace, Babbage) and Turing (died 1954) are fine.
- Some later floors need invented homage characters, which also gives more creative freedom.
